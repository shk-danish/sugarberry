Our Story

About Sugarberry

Born in Pakistan, made for every skin tone. We believe glow is not a privilege — it's your right.

How It Started

Crafted with Love 💕

Sugarberry was born from a simple frustration — finding clean, effective skincare that actually works for South Asian skin. Our founder, Zara, spent years testing imported products that promised glow but left behind irritation.

In 2022, she started formulating in her kitchen — blending rose extracts, local honey, and time-tested herbs into products that her friends immediately fell in love with. Today, Sugarberry ships across all of Pakistan.

Every formula is clean, cruelty-free, and dermatologist-tested. We use no harmful chemicals — just nature's finest, bottled beautifully.
